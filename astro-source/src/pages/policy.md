# Legal, Research, and Editorial Policy
**Version 1.0 — Draft for Pre-Launch Review**

## Purpose
Star Nootropics is a research-forward wellness and educational platform devoted to the critical study of cognition, memory, learning, neuroscience, nootropics, nutritional supplements, botanicals, human-animal relationships, psychology, and related areas of scientific inquiry.

Star operates on a principle of intellectual independence: commercial interests do not determine research conclusions.

The website may contain scholarly articles, literature reviews, historical archives, educational materials, affiliate relationships, and a retail store. These functions must remain distinguishable from one another so readers can determine when they are reading independent educational material and when they are viewing commercial content.

This policy is an internal editorial and compliance standard. Before Star begins manufacturing, repackaging, labeling, or directly selling dietary supplements, applicable product labels, business practices, and regulatory obligations should receive professional legal and regulatory review.

## Research Independence
Star may research any lawful academic subject appropriate for serious scholarly inquiry, including substances that Star does not and cannot sell.

- The presence of a compound in the Star Research Library does not mean that Star recommends, endorses, distributes, or sells that compound.
- Research concerning piracetam, Noopept, bromantane, Semax, Selank, LSD, psilocybin, other psychedelics, pharmaceutical substances, investigational compounds, or controlled substances may be published for legitimate historical, scientific, psychological, pharmacological, or educational purposes.
- Such articles must remain educational. They must not function as instructions for obtaining illegal substances, circumventing prescription requirements, evading drug laws, or manufacturing controlled substances.

## Separation of Research and Commerce
Star will maintain a visible distinction between the Research Library and the Star Shop.

- A research monograph will not become a product advertisement merely because the subject is commercially interesting.
- Research-only substances will not contain purchase buttons, ordering instructions, pricing, product inventory, or links directing readers to sources selling restricted, controlled, prescription-only, or unapproved drugs.
- Commercial pages may link to appropriate Star educational material when necessary to explain ingredients or evidence, but the resulting presentation must not convert a research discussion into an unsupported advertising claim.
- When scientific evidence concerning a product is mixed, weak, negative, or inconclusive, Star will state that conclusion even when Star financially benefits from sales of the product.

## Products Offered for Sale
The Star Shop will offer only products determined to be lawful for the intended form of sale and jurisdiction. The fact that a substance has historically been called a "nootropic," is available from another website, or is unscheduled under federal law does not guarantee commercial eligibility.
